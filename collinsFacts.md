## 5. Copy rules (T-Mobile compliance)

1. **Always name the plan with a price** — e.g. "T-Mobile **Rely** Home Internet $35/mo". Never a bare "$35/mo".
2. **Always show the asterisk legal** with a price: "\*With AutoPay and eligible voice line, plus taxes & fees."
3. **Never say "gift card"** — always "**virtual prepaid Mastercard®**" (e.g. "Get $100 back via virtual prepaid Mastercard®").
4. Don't use: "$0 Due Today" ($35 device connection charge is due at sale), "Free 5G Wi-Fi Equipment" (use "No Equipment Fee"), "Fast & Free Shipping", "No credit card needed", invented reviews/ratings or fake urgency.
5. Every bullet list of offers must be followed by the fine-print paragraph (section 6) with "See Full Terms" popups.
