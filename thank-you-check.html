A T-Mobile Home Internet specialist will confirm your eligibility, review the available offers and pricing with you, answer your questions, and help you complete your order. Call 888-999-2819.

How does the 15-day test drive work?

New and existing T-Mobile customers activating Home Internet on a qualifying plan can get their money back via a one-time bill credit if they schedule cancellation within the first 15 days of activation. The gateway must be returned.

Is there an annual contract?

No. There’s no annual contract. If you cancel, you’ll need to return the gateway undamaged or pay up to $370.

Are taxes and fees included in the monthly price?

No. T-Mobile Rely Home Internet is $35/mo with AutoPay and eligible voice line, plus taxes & fees. Without AutoPay it’s $10 more per line per month, and a $35 device connection charge is due at sale.
