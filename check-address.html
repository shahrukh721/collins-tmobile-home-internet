Common questions

Real Questions. Clearer Answers.

Is T-Mobile 5G Home Internet available at my address?

Availability depends on your service address and network capacity, which changes over time. Enter your information in the form above, or call 888-765-4506, and we’ll check your address for you.

Is there an annual contract?

No. There’s no annual contract. If you cancel, you’ll need to return the gateway undamaged or pay up to $370.

Can I switch from my current internet provider?

Yes. If T-Mobile 5G Home Internet is available at your address, you can switch. Many people keep their current service until their gateway is set up, then cancel their old provider.

How does setup work?

Your T-Mobile 5G gateway is a simple self-install: plug it in at the service address you provide at activation and follow the step-by-step instructions. Service is for in-home use at that address.

Do I need a T-Mobile phone service to qualify?

T-Mobile Rely Home Internet at $35/mo requires AutoPay and an eligible voice line, plus taxes & fees, qualifying credit, and a new Home Internet line. Our team can tell you which options fit your account.

What does the 15-day test drive mean?

New and existing T-Mobile customers activating Home Internet on a qualifying plan can get their money back via a one-time bill credit if they schedule cancellation within the first 15 days of activation. The gateway must be returned.

Are taxes and fees included in the monthly price?

No. T-Mobile Rely Home Internet is $35/mo with AutoPay and eligible voice line, plus taxes & fees. Without AutoPay it’s $10 more per line per month, and a $35 device connection charge is due at sale.

What information do I need to check availability?

Just your name, mobile phone number, email, and the street address and ZIP code where you want service. It’s free to check and there’s no obligation.
